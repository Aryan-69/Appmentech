How We Work

From idea to store listing

Store review, device fragmentation and release management are where mobile projects usually slip. We plan for all three at the start.

1. Discovery

We start with the business problem, the users and the constraints — not with a technology choice. You get a written scope before anyone writes code.

2. Architecture & design

Product architecture, data model, integrations and user experience are designed up front, so the build has no surprises in week six.

3. Build in increments

Two-week increments with a working, reviewable deployment at the end of each one. You see progress in a browser, not in a status report.

4. Quality engineering

Automated tests, code review and security checks run in CI on every change. Quality is part of the build, not a phase at the end.

5. Launch

Production deployment with monitoring, logging, backups and rollback in place from day one.

6. Support & iterate

Post-launch support, performance tuning and a roadmap for the next set of features.

FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask Us

How much does it cost to build a mobile app in India?

Cost tracks scope, not screens: authentication, payments, offline sync and integrations drive far more effort than page count. We scope your app in a discovery session and quote a fixed price against that written scope.

How long does it take to build an app?

A focused MVP is a much shorter build than a full consumer product with payments and social features. We give you a dated plan after discovery, and you see a working build every two weeks from then on.

Should I build native or cross-platform?

Cross-platform is usually right when your app is largely forms, lists and API calls, because it halves long-term maintenance. Native is worth it when you depend on platform-specific hardware, performance or newest OS features. We recommend one based on your product, not on what we prefer to build.

Do you publish the app to the App Store and Google Play?

Yes. We prepare the store listings, assets and privacy declarations, submit under your developer accounts, and handle any review feedback.

Who owns the app and the source code?

You do. The repositories, developer accounts, signing keys and backend infrastructure are registered in your name and handed over.

Can you take over an app another team built?

Yes. We start with an audit of the codebase, dependencies, build pipeline and store status, then give you a written view of what it will take to stabilise it before we quote further work.

Ready to build your app?

Bring us a product idea or an existing app that has stalled. We will tell you honestly what it takes to get it into the store.
