How We Work

Stabilise, automate, then optimise

We work in that order deliberately. Optimising a system nobody can deploy reliably just makes the outages cheaper.

1. Review

Architecture, security posture, deployment process, monitoring and cost, assessed against how the system is actually used.

2. Stabilise

Backups, alerting, access control and a rollback path — the things you need before you change anything else.

3. Codify

Infrastructure moved into code and version control, environments made reproducible.

4. Automate delivery

Build, test and deploy wired into a pipeline with quality gates and staged rollout.

5. Optimise cost

Right-sizing, storage tiering, scaling policies and reserved capacity, with the savings measured against last month's bill.

6. Hand over

Runbooks, dashboards and documentation, plus training so your team operates it confidently.

FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask Us

Should we move to the cloud at all?

Not always. If your workload is steady, predictable and already running fine, migration may cost more than it returns. We look at your traffic pattern, compliance requirements and operational burden before recommending a move.

How do you reduce our cloud bill?

Right-sizing over-provisioned resources, tiering storage, fixing scaling policies that never scale down, removing orphaned resources and committing to reserved capacity where usage is stable. We measure the result against your previous bill rather than promising a percentage upfront.

Can you migrate without downtime?

In most cases yes, using staged migration with traffic shifted gradually and a rollback path at each step. Where a short maintenance window is genuinely needed, we tell you upfront rather than discovering it during the cutover.

Which cloud do you work with?

AWS is our primary platform and where our depth is. We work with other providers where you are already committed to them.

Do you provide ongoing operations support?

Yes — monitoring, patching, incident response and cost review on an agreed schedule. We also set teams up to run it themselves, with runbooks and training, when that is what you want.

What is infrastructure as code and why does it matter?

It means your servers, networks and permissions are defined in files kept in version control rather than clicked together in a console. It matters because environments become reproducible, changes become reviewable, and a lost environment is a rebuild rather than a catastrophe.

Cloud bill rising faster than your traffic?

Tell us what you are running today. We will review the architecture, the spend and the deployment process, and show you what to change first.
