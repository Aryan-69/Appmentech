CRM Development

Custom CRM Software, Built Around How You Actually Sell

Most businesses do not lose deals because they lack a CRM. They lose them because the CRM they pay for does not match their process, so the real work goes back into a spreadsheet and a WhatsApp group. We build the one that fits.

Talk About Your Process See Our Work
The Problem

The spreadsheet is the real CRM

If any of these sound familiar, the issue is fit, not discipline. Software people have to work around is software they eventually stop using.

Enquiries arrive in five places

Website form, Instagram DM, a missed call, a walk-in and a WhatsApp forward — and nobody can say which ones were answered.

Follow-ups depend on memory

The deal that goes cold is rarely the one that said no. It is the one nobody called back on Thursday.

Per-seat pricing punishes growth

You start limiting who gets a login, which quietly guarantees the data will be incomplete.

Reporting means rebuilding a sheet

Someone spends Monday morning assembling the same numbers by hand, and they are stale by Tuesday.

The website and the CRM are strangers

A lead fills a form, an email arrives, and a human retypes it. That is the gap where leads die.

Everyone can see everything

No roles, no permissions, no audit trail — and no safe way to give a new joiner access.

What We Build

The parts of a CRM that earn their place

We do not build every module in the brochure. We build the ones your business runs on, and leave room for the rest.

Lead capture and routing

Website forms, landing pages, ad leads, calls and walk-ins land in one place and get assigned to a named person automatically.

Pipelines that match your stages

Your stages, your qualification rules, your lost reasons — not a template borrowed from a software company in another country.

Customer and account records

One record per customer with the full history: enquiries, calls, quotations, invoices, tickets and notes.

Quotations, invoices and payments

Generate a quotation, convert it to an invoice, attach a payment link and see what is actually collected.

Follow-up automation

Tasks, reminders and escalations that fire on their own, plus WhatsApp, email and SMS templates your team can send in one click.

Roles, permissions and audit trail

Sales sees their own pipeline, a manager sees the branch, the owner sees everything — and every change is logged.

Dashboards worth opening

Conversion by source, ageing enquiries, revenue by stage and per-person activity, live rather than assembled by hand.

Migration from what you have now

Spreadsheets, an old CRM or a mix of both — mapped, trial-imported, checked by you, then migrated for real.

Integrations with the rest of your stack

Your website, payment gateway, shipping partner, accounting tool, email and the WhatsApp Business API, joined up as one system.

How We Work

From your process to a working CRM in six steps

The first deliverable is not code. It is a written map of how your business actually handles a customer, which is usually the first time anyone has drawn it.

1. Map the process

We sit with the people who use it and write down how an enquiry becomes a customer today, including the parts that happen on paper.

2. Design the data model

Records, stages, roles and permissions are designed up front, because that is the decision that is expensive to change later.

3. Build the core first

Leads, pipeline and customer records ship first, so your team can use it while the rest is being built.

4. Migrate and verify

A trial import you check yourself, then the real migration, with the original export kept untouched as a fallback.

5. Train and launch

A walkthrough for the team, a short written guide, and a launch with backups and monitoring already in place.

6. Support and extend

A care plan for changes, plus the next set of features scoped once you have used it for a month and know what you want.

FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask Us

Should I subscribe to a CRM product or have one built?

If a standard CRM fits the way you already work, subscribe to one — we will tell you so and save you the money. Building makes sense when your process does not fit the boxes a product gives you, when per-seat pricing is growing faster than your team, or when the CRM has to work as one system with your website, billing and operations.

How much does a custom CRM cost?

It depends on how many roles, stages and integrations it has to cover. A focused CRM for one team is a very different build from a multi-branch system with role-based access and billing. We scope it in writing first and then quote a fixed price against that scope, so there is no open-ended hourly meter.

Can you move our data out of spreadsheets or our current CRM?

Yes, and it is usually the first milestone. We map your existing columns and records to the new data model, run a trial import you can check, and only then run the real migration. You keep the original export untouched as a fallback.

Will it work with our website, billing and WhatsApp?

That is normally the point of building it. Website enquiry forms, payment gateways, invoicing or accounting tools, email, SMS and the WhatsApp Business API are the integrations we are asked for most, and they are scoped in from the start rather than bolted on later.

Who owns the CRM and the data in it?

You do, entirely. Source code, database, hosting accounts and third-party subscriptions are registered in your name and handed over. There is no per-seat licence and no platform you cannot leave.

What happens when we want to change it later?

Small changes are covered by a monthly care plan; larger changes are scoped and quoted like any other project. Because you own the code, you are also free to have someone else do it — we hand over documentation on the assumption that you might.

Tell us how a customer moves through your business

That conversation is usually enough for us to tell you whether you need a custom CRM or just a better use of one you already pay for. Either answer is free.
