What We Do

How We Work

Fix the feedback loop first

We do not start by writing hundreds of tests. We start by finding out how long it takes your team to learn that something broke, and shortening that.

1. Assess

A short audit of your current coverage, pipeline, environments and defect history — where bugs escape and why.

2. Prioritise by risk

Automation effort goes first to the flows that lose money when they break, not to whatever is easiest to script.

3. Build the framework

A test architecture your own developers can extend: readable, stable, no hidden waits, no magic.

4. Wire into CI

Tests run on every commit with clear pass/fail signals and no flaky tests tolerated — a flaky test is a bug we fix.

5. Cover the regressions

Suite expanded steadily until a release no longer needs a manual regression round.

6. Hand over

Documentation, walkthroughs and pairing so your team owns the suite. We are not trying to become permanent.

FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask Us

What is the difference between QA and test automation?

QA is the whole discipline of preventing and detecting defects — strategy, process, environments and release criteria. Test automation is one tool inside it. Automating a broken process just produces failures faster, so we look at both.

How long before automated testing pays off?

Usually as soon as the first risk-based regression suite runs in CI, because it replaces a manual round that was consuming days per release. We prioritise the highest-value flows first specifically to make that payback early.

Can you work with our existing test suite?

Yes. We audit what exists, keep what is trustworthy, fix what is flaky and replace what is unmaintainable — rather than deleting your team's work and starting over on principle.

Do you do manual testing as well?

Yes, where it belongs: exploratory testing, usability checks and first-run validation of new features. Repetitive regression checking is what we automate.

Do you test mobile apps and APIs too?

Yes. API and integration coverage is usually where we start, because it is faster and catches more defects per test than UI automation, and we test mobile builds across device and OS combinations.

Will our developers be able to maintain the suite after you leave?

That is the goal we design for. The framework is written to be readable, we document it, and we pair with your engineers during handover so ownership actually transfers.

Shipping slower than you want to?

Most teams do not have a testing problem — they have a feedback-speed problem. Tell us where releases hurt and we will show you what to automate first.
