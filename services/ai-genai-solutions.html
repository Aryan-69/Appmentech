How We Work

Prove it small, then scale it

AI work is measured differently from ordinary software: correctness is statistical. Our process reflects that.

1. Pick one workflow

We choose a single process with a clear cost — hours spent, tickets handled, errors made — so the result is measurable in your numbers.

2. Build the evaluation set

Before the system is built, we assemble real examples with known-correct answers. This is what tells us whether it works.

3. Prototype

A working prototype against your real data, usually narrow on purpose, so you can judge quality rather than a demo script.

4. Harden

Guardrails, permissions, PII handling, rate limits, logging and fallbacks for when the model is unavailable or wrong.

5. Integrate

Wired into the systems your team already uses, so nobody has to learn a new tool to get the benefit.

6. Monitor

Accuracy, cost per request and latency tracked in production, with alerts when quality drifts.

FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask Us

What can generative AI actually do for my business?

The reliable wins are narrow and repetitive: answering questions from your own documents, extracting fields from paperwork, drafting first-pass replies, classifying and routing incoming work. We start by finding a workflow where the current cost is measurable, so the improvement is measurable too.

Will my company data be used to train someone else's model?

Not in the deployments we build. We configure model providers with training disabled, or run in your own cloud account where data residency requires it, and we document exactly what leaves your environment.

How do you know the AI is giving correct answers?

We build an evaluation set of real questions with known-correct answers before the system is built, and score every change against it. You get an accuracy figure and a way to keep measuring it in production.

Which AI model do you use?

Whichever scores best on your evaluation set within your cost, latency and data-residency constraints. We keep the model behind an abstraction so it can be swapped as the market moves.

Do I need my own data ready before starting?

Not organised, but it needs to exist. Documents, tickets, product data or transcripts are enough to start; part of our work is turning that into a usable retrieval pipeline.

What does an AI project cost to run?

There are two costs: the build, which we quote fixed against scope, and the per-request inference cost, which we estimate during the prototype so you know the running cost before rollout.

Want to know if AI fits your business?

Bring us the process you would like to automate. We will tell you whether an AI system is the right tool for it — including when it is not.
