What We Build

The store, and the operations behind it

The storefront is the easy half. What decides whether an e-commerce business works is order management, stock accuracy, payment reconciliation and returns — the parts a template rarely handles well once volume arrives.

Product catalogue

Variants, pricing rules, categories, search and filtering that stay fast as the catalogue grows.

Cart & checkout

Checkout designed to reduce abandonment, with saved carts, guest checkout and payment retries that recover failed transactions.

Payments

Payment gateway integration, refunds and reconciliation, built to survive the failure modes real payment flows have.

Order management

Order lifecycle, fulfilment status, shipping integration and customer notifications in one place.

Customer accounts

Order history, saved addresses, wishlists and self-service returns that cut support volume.

Returns & refunds

A returns workflow that keeps stock and finance in sync instead of leaving reconciliation to a spreadsheet.

How We Work

Launch small, then scale the operations

We get you selling with the smallest complete order-to-delivery loop, then extend it as volume exposes what actually needs automating.

1. Discovery

We start with the business problem, the users and the constraints — not with a technology choice. You get a written scope before anyone writes code.

2. Architecture & design

Product architecture, data model, integrations and user experience are designed up front, so the build has no surprises in week six.

3. Build in increments

Two-week increments with a working, reviewable deployment at the end of each one. You see progress in a browser, not in a status report.

4. Quality engineering

Automated tests, code review and security checks run in CI on every change. Quality is part of the build, not a phase at the end.

5. Launch

Production deployment with monitoring, logging, backups and rollback in place from day one.

6. Support & iterate

Post-launch support, performance tuning and a roadmap for the next set of features.

FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask Us

Should I use Shopify or build a custom e-commerce platform?

Hosted platforms are the right answer for a straightforward catalogue and standard checkout — they are cheaper and faster to launch. Custom becomes worth it when your pricing rules, fulfilment workflow, integrations or transaction fees stop fitting the template. We will tell you honestly which side of that line you are on.

How long does it take to launch an online store?

The first sellable version — catalogue, cart, checkout, payments and order management — is a much shorter build than the full operations suite. We launch the complete order-to-delivery loop first and add automation as volume justifies it.

Can you integrate payments, shipping and accounting?

Yes. Payment gateways, shipping providers, ERP and accounting systems are standard parts of the builds we do, including the reconciliation and retry logic those integrations need.

Will my product pages rank in Google?

They are built to: server-rendered pages, clean URLs, unique titles and descriptions, product structured data, fast loading and a valid sitemap. Ranking also depends on your catalogue depth and competition, which we will be realistic with you about.

Can you migrate my existing store?

Yes — products, customers, orders and URLs. URL mapping and redirects are planned in advance so you do not lose the search rankings you already have.

Do you handle high-traffic sale events?

Yes. We load-test against a modelled peak, set scaling policies ahead of the event, and put monitoring in place so problems surface in minutes rather than in customer complaints.

Selling online, or planning to?

Whether you are launching a first store or outgrowing a template platform, tell us what you sell and how you sell it. We will tell you what to build.
