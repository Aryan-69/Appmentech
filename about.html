About Us

An Engineering Team That Ships, Supports and Stays Accountable

Appmentech Technologies builds custom web, mobile, AI and cloud software for growing businesses — and stays responsible for it after launch.

Who We Are

Appmentech Technologies is an India-based software engineering team working with clients in India and abroad. We design and build the systems businesses run on: customer-facing websites and mobile applications, SaaS products, e-commerce platforms, internal tools, cloud infrastructure and the automation that connects them.

We deliberately work as one accountable team rather than a chain of specialists handing work between departments. The people who scope your project are the people who build it, test it and deploy it. That means there is no gap between what was promised in a proposal and what an engineer understood three weeks later — a gap that quietly ruins more software projects than any technical decision does.

Our deepest discipline is quality engineering. Testing, automation and release quality are not a phase we run before launch; they are how we build. That background shapes everything else we do, including how we approach newer work like AI systems, where the hard part is not building a demonstration but proving the thing is reliably correct.

How We Work

Principles We Actually Apply

These are not values on a wall. Each one changes a decision we make during delivery.

Business first

We start with the business problem, the users and the constraints — not with a technology we already wanted to use.

User first

Every product is designed around the people who will actually use it, including the staff who use the unglamorous internal half.

Quality first

Automated tests, code review and security checks run on every change in CI, so defects are caught in minutes rather than in production.

Security first

Identity, encryption, access control and data handling are designed at the start, when they are still cheap to get right.

Automation first

Repetitive build, test, deployment and operational work gets automated, because manual steps are the ones that get skipped under pressure.

AI ready

We build platforms with clean data and clear interfaces, so AI can be added when the business case is real rather than bolted on as a demo.

What We Build

Our work spans six main areas, each with its own page explaining what we deliver, how we work and what it involves:

  • Web development — corporate sites, portals, e-commerce, SaaS applications and PWAs.
  • Mobile app development — Android, iOS and cross-platform apps from MVP to store launch.
  • AI and GenAI solutions — RAG assistants, document automation and AI agents, delivered with evaluation and guardrails.
  • Software testing and quality engineering — automation frameworks, CI quality gates and release readiness.
  • Cloud and DevOps — architecture, migration, infrastructure as code and deployment pipelines.
  • E-commerce platforms — catalogue, checkout, payments, order management and returns.

You can see how this works in practice in the Cookrie case study — a custom e-commerce platform we designed, built and shipped end to end.

How We Engage

Every engagement starts with a free consultation and a written scope. We quote a fixed price against that scope, so you know the cost before the build starts rather than discovering it through an hourly meter. Work then proceeds in two-week increments, each ending in a deployment you can open in a browser and judge for yourself.

Everything we build belongs to you: source code, repositories, cloud accounts, domains and third-party subscriptions are registered in your name and handed over. We are glad to sign your NDA before the first detailed technical conversation, and to work under your master services agreement and security requirements.
