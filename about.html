About Us

An Engineering Team That Ships, Supports and Stays Accountable

Appmentech Technologies builds custom websites, customer portals and CRM systems for Indian businesses — and stays responsible for them after launch.

Who We Are

Appmentech Technologies is an India-based software engineering team working with clients in India and abroad. We build two things properly rather than eight things vaguely: web applications — marketing sites, customer portals, e-commerce platforms and internal tools — and custom CRM systems that fit the way a business actually sells and serves, instead of the way a subscription product assumes it does.

We deliberately work as one accountable team rather than a chain of specialists handing work between departments. The people who scope your project are the people who build it, test it and deploy it. That means there is no gap between what was promised in a proposal and what an engineer understood three weeks later — a gap that quietly ruins more software projects than any technical decision does.

Our deepest discipline is quality engineering. Testing, automation and release quality are not a phase we run before launch; they are how we build. It is also why we are candid about scope: if a subscription CRM or an off-the-shelf template would serve you better than a custom build, we will say so before you spend anything.

How We Work

Principles We Actually Apply

These are not values on a wall. Each one changes a decision we make during delivery.

Business first

We start with the business problem, the users and the constraints — not with a technology we already wanted to use.

User first

Every product is designed around the people who will actually use it, including the staff who use the unglamorous internal half.

Quality first

Automated tests, code review and security checks run on every change in CI, so defects are caught in minutes rather than in production.

Security first

Identity, encryption, access control and data handling are designed at the start, when they are still cheap to get right.

Automation first

Repetitive build, test, deployment and operational work gets automated, because manual steps are the ones that get skipped under pressure.

Yours to keep

Code, repositories, domains and third-party accounts are registered in your name from day one, so leaving us is always an option you have.

What We Build

Three pages cover what we deliver, how we work and what each one involves:

  • Web development — corporate sites, customer portals, internal tools and progressive web applications.
  • CRM development — lead capture, pipelines, customer records, follow-up automation, roles and reporting.
  • E-commerce platforms — catalogue, checkout, payments, shipping, order management and returns.

We also take on software testing and release automation, cloud and DevOps work, and API integration — usually for clients we already build for, or through agency partners. Ask, and we will tell you honestly whether it is a fit.

You can see how this works in practice in our recent projects — including Cookrie, a custom e-commerce platform we designed, built and shipped end to end.

How We Engage

Every engagement starts with a conversation and a written scope. We quote a fixed price against that scope, so you know the cost before the build starts rather than discovering it through an hourly meter. Work then proceeds in two-week increments, each ending in a deployment you can open in a browser and judge for yourself.

Everything we build belongs to you: source code, repositories, cloud accounts, domains and third-party subscriptions are registered in your name and handed over. We are glad to sign your NDA before the first detailed technical conversation, and to work under your master services agreement and security requirements.
