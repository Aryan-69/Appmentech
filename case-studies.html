E-Commerce & Retail

Cookrie — a custom e-commerce platform, built end to end

Cookrie is an online retail store that needed to sell directly to its customers without renting a hosted platform, paying per-transaction fees, or accepting the limits a template storefront places on pricing, fulfilment and returns. We designed and built the whole platform — storefront, checkout, accounts and the operational tooling behind it.

The problem

Template storefronts are excellent until your operations stop fitting them. The parts that decide whether a retail business runs smoothly are rarely on the product page: keeping stock accurate when several people order the same item at once, recovering a payment that failed halfway through, and processing a return without a member of staff reconciling stock and refunds by hand afterwards. Cookrie needed those parts built properly, and needed to own the platform rather than rent it.

What we built

  • Product catalogue — categories, variants and pricing, with search and filtering that stay fast as the catalogue grows.
  • Shopping cart and checkout — a mobile-first checkout flow designed to minimise abandonment, with saved carts and payment retries.
  • User accounts — registration, order history, saved addresses and self-service order tracking, which cuts routine support requests.
  • Order management — the full order lifecycle from placement through fulfilment and delivery, with customer notifications at each stage.
  • Returns and refunds — a returns workflow that keeps stock counts and refund records in sync instead of leaving reconciliation to a spreadsheet.

How we approached it

We launched the smallest complete order-to-delivery loop first — catalogue, cart, checkout, payment and fulfilment — so the store could sell while the operational tooling was extended around it. Everything went out through a CI pipeline with automated tests running on every change, and the checkout and catalogue were load-tested against realistic peak traffic before launch rather than during a sale.

Search visibility was part of the build rather than a later retrofit: server-rendered product and category pages, clean URLs, unique titles and descriptions per product, structured data and a valid sitemap.

The result

Cookrie runs today as an independent, fully owned e-commerce platform at cookrie.in — no per-transaction platform fee, no ceiling on customisation, and an operations workflow that matches how the business actually works.
